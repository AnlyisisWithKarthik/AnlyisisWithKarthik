# 📄 Executive Summary: slice Smart Budgets

> **One-page pitch for stakeholders**

---

## The Opportunity

**72% of young Indians (18-28) experience financial anxiety from overspending** — yet no payment app in India offers an intelligent, engaging budgeting experience for this demographic.

slice, with **15M+ users and rich transaction data**, is uniquely positioned to solve this with **Smart Budgets** — an AI-powered, gamified budgeting layer built natively into the app.
