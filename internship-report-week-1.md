## 3. Finance Department Overview
The finance function is divided into three key sections:

1. **Accounts Receivable (AR):**
   - Handles customer billing, collections, and incoming payments.
   - Ensures timely receipt of revenue from guests and corporate clients.

2. **Accounts Payable (AP):**
   - Manages vendor payments, invoices, and expense processing.
   - Ensures proper approval and scheduling of outgoing payments.

3. **Compliance:**
   - Handles statutory and regulatory requirements.
   - Responsible for tax filings and legal compliance, including **GST, VAT**, and other applicable taxes.
