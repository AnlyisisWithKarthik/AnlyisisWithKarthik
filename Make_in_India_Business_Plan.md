## 1. Executive Summary

**SuryaTech Solar Solutions Pvt. Ltd.** is a proposed solar energy manufacturing and solutions company aligned with India's "Make in India" initiative under the **Renewable Energy** focus sector. Headquartered in Hyderabad, Telangana, the company will manufacture high-efficiency solar photovoltaic (PV) modules and provide end-to-end solar energy solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial customers across India.

**Vision:** To become a leading Indian manufacturer of affordable, high-efficiency solar energy products, contributing to India's goal of 500 GW non-fossil fuel energy capacity by 2030.

**Mission:** To design, manufacture, and deploy world-class solar energy solutions that are made in India, empowering businesses and households to transition to clean energy while creating local employment and reducing import dependence.

## 2. Company Description

### 2.1 About the Company

SuryaTech Solar Solutions Pvt. Ltd. will be incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 as a Private Limited Company. The company operates in the renewable energy sector with a focus on solar photovoltaic technology — one of the 25 focus sectors under the Government of India's "Make in India" program.

### 2.2 Make in India Alignment

The "Make in India" initiative, launched in September 2014, aims to transform India into a global manufacturing hub. The Renewable Energy sector is a cornerstone of this initiative with the following government support:

- **Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme:** ₹19,500 Crore allocated for solar PV module manufacturing.
- **ALMM Requirement:** Only domestically manufactured modules can be used in government-subsidized projects.
- **Basic Customs Duty (BCD):** 40% on imported solar modules and 25% on imported solar cells (effective April 2022).
- **National Solar Mission:** Target of 280 GW solar capacity by 2030.
- **PM-KUSUM Scheme:** Solar adoption in agriculture sector.
- **Rooftop Solar Programme:** Target of 40 GW rooftop solar installations.

### 2.3 Business Model

SuryaTech operates a **hybrid B2B and B2C model**:

1. **Manufacturing (B2B):** Produce solar PV modules (monocrystalline PERC and TOPCon technology) and sell to EPC contractors, project developers, and system integrators.
2. **Solar Solutions (B2B + B2C):** Provide turnkey rooftop solar installation services for residential, commercial, and industrial (C&I) customers.
3. **After-Sales & O&M (B2C/B2B):** Annual maintenance contracts for installed systems, ensuring long-term customer relationships and recurring revenue.

### 2.4 Legal & Regulatory Compliance

- Registered under Companies Act, 2013
- GST Registration
- MSME / Udyam Registration
- Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) certification for solar modules
- IEC 61215 / IEC 61730 international certifications
- ALMM listing with Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE)
- Factory license and environmental clearances

## 3. Market Analysis

### 3.1 Industry Overview

India's solar energy sector is one of the fastest-growing in the world:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| India's installed solar capacity (2024) | ~82 GW |
| India's 2030 solar target | 280 GW |
| Annual solar installation rate | 15–25 GW/year |
| Domestic module manufacturing capacity (2024) | ~40 GW |
| Domestic cell manufacturing capacity (2024) | ~7 GW |
| Solar module market size (India, 2024) | ~₹60,000 Crore |
| Expected CAGR (2024–2030) | 25–30% |

### 3.2 Target Market Segments

#### Segment 1: Utility-Scale Solar Projects (B2B)
- Large solar parks (50 MW–500 MW)
- Government tenders (SECI, NTPC, State DISCOMs)
- Private IPPs (Independent Power Producers)
- **Market Size:** ~₹35,000 Crore annually

#### Segment 2: Commercial & Industrial (C&I) Rooftop (B2B)
- Factories, warehouses, IT parks, hospitals, hotels
- Open access and net metering arrangements
- Typical system size: 50 kW – 5 MW
- **Market Size:** ~₹12,000 Crore annually

#### Segment 3: Residential Rooftop Solar (B2C)
- Individual homeowners
- Housing societies and gated communities
- PM Surya Ghar (Muft Bijli Yojana) subsidy beneficiaries
- Typical system size: 2 kW – 10 kW
- **Market Size:** ~₹8,000 Crore annually

#### Segment 4: Agricultural Solar (B2B/B2C)
- Solar water pumps
- PM-KUSUM scheme beneficiaries
- **Market Size:** ~₹5,000 Crore annually

### 3.3 Market Trends

1. **Import substitution:** Government policy is shifting demand from imported (China) to domestically manufactured modules.
2. **Technology upgrade:** Market moving from polycrystalline to monocrystalline PERC, and next to TOPCon and HJT technologies.
3. **Increasing efficiency:** Module efficiencies are crossing 22%+ at commercial scale.
4. **Bifacial modules:** Growing demand for bifacial modules in utility-scale projects (10–15% additional energy yield).
5. **Energy storage integration:** Solar-plus-storage systems becoming mainstream.
6. **Green hydrogen:** Future demand driver for large-scale solar installations.
7. **Corporate ESG mandates:** Driving C&I solar adoption.

### 3.4 Customer Persona

| Customer Type | Key Need | Decision Factor |
|---|---|---|
| EPC Contractor | Reliable supply, ALMM-listed modules | Price, delivery, warranty |
| C&I Business Owner | Reduce electricity cost by 30–50% | ROI, payback period, reliability |
| Residential Homeowner | Lower bills, government subsidy | Subsidy, brand trust, after-sales |
| Farmer | Reduce diesel pump costs | Government scheme, ease of use |

### 4.2 Competitive Positioning — SWOT Analysis

#### Strengths
- **Technology Focus:** Start with latest TOPCon technology (higher efficiency than competitors' legacy PERC lines).
- **Location Advantage:** Telangana offers excellent solar irradiation, logistics connectivity, and state government incentives (TS-iPASS, power subsidies).
- **Lean Operations:** Smaller scale enables agile decision-making and customization for C&I/residential segments.
- **End-to-End Model:** Manufacturing + installation + O&M creates a full value chain.
- **Data-Driven Approach:** Leverage IoT and analytics for system monitoring, predictive maintenance, and customer insights.

#### Weaknesses
- **New Entrant:** No established brand or track record.
- **Scale Disadvantage:** Cannot match per-unit costs of GW-scale manufacturers initially.
- **Capital Intensive:** Solar manufacturing requires significant upfront investment.

#### Opportunities
- **PLI Scheme:** Access to government incentives for domestic manufacturing.
- **ALMM Mandate:** Imported modules blocked from subsidized projects, creating guaranteed domestic demand.
- **Telangana State Incentives:** Land, power, and tax benefits under the state industrial policy.
- **Underserved C&I/Residential Segment:** Large manufacturers focus on utility; mid-market is underserved.
- **Technology Leap:** Starting fresh with TOPCon/HJT avoids legacy equipment costs.

#### Threats
- **Price Competition:** Chinese manufacturers dominate globally on cost.
- **Policy Changes:** Government policy shifts could reduce protections.
- **Technology Risk:** Rapid technology changes could make current investments obsolete.
- **Raw Material Prices:** Silicon wafer and silver paste prices are volatile.

### 4.3 Competitive Advantage — Differentiation Strategy

SuryaTech differentiates through:

1. **Technology Leadership:** Manufacturing with N-type TOPCon cells (22.5%+ module efficiency) while most Indian manufacturers still produce P-type PERC (20–21% efficiency).
2. **Regional Focus:** Dominate the South India (Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu) market with local presence and faster delivery.
3. **Full-Stack Service:** Module manufacturing + design + installation + monitoring + maintenance — one-stop shop for C&I and residential customers.
4. **Smart Solar Platform:** IoT-based monitoring dashboard for all installed systems, powered by data analytics (a unique differentiator leveraging the founding team's analytics expertise).

### 10.3 Exit Strategy

SuryaTech provides multiple exit options for investors:

#### Option 1: Strategic Acquisition (Preferred, Year 5–7)
- **Acquirers:** Large Indian conglomerates expanding into solar (e.g., Reliance, JSW, Adani) or international solar companies entering India.
- **Valuation Basis:** 2–3x revenue or 15–20x EBITDA.
- **Target Exit Valuation:** ₹500–800 Crore (based on projected Year 5 revenue of ₹250+ Crore).

#### Option 2: Initial Public Offering — IPO (Year 6–8)
- List on BSE/NSE (SME exchange initially, then main board).
- Solar manufacturing companies in India are seeing strong market interest (e.g., Waaree Energies IPO 2024).
- **Target Valuation at IPO:** ₹800–1,200 Crore.

#### Option 3: Secondary Sale / Private Equity Buyout (Year 4–6)
- Sell stake to a larger PE/growth fund.
- Provides liquidity to early investors while company continues to grow.
- **Target Return:** 5–8x for seed/angel investors; 3–5x for Series A investors.

#### Option 4: Management Buyout (MBO)
- Founding team buys out investor stakes using company cash flows and debt.
- Applicable if company is profitable and founders wish to retain full control.
