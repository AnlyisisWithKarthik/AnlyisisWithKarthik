# 🍕 slice Smart Budgets — Product Case Study

**Empowering Young Indians to Spend Smarter with AI-Driven Budgeting**

> A comprehensive Product Management case study for the **slice PM Internship**, demonstrating user research, market analysis, product strategy, and data-driven decision making.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

India's Gen-Z and young millennials (18–28) are adopting digital payments at unprecedented rates, yet **72% report anxiety about overspending** and lack tools that speak their language. slice already owns a powerful position in this demographic through its payment card and UPI integration.

**slice Smart Budgets** proposes an intelligent, gamified budgeting layer built natively into the slice app that:
- Automatically categorizes spending using ML on transaction data
- Delivers proactive "nudges" before users overshoot budgets
- Gamifies saving with streaks, badges, and social challenges
- Provides weekly "Money Mood" reports in a Gen-Z-friendly format

**Projected Impact:** +18% DAU engagement, +12% transaction frequency, 25% reduction in support tickets related to billing confusion, and a powerful data moat for personalized financial products.
